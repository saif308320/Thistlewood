Since 1998

A Leeds shop that still smells of beeswax.

Margaret Thistlewood opened a unit on Kirkstall Road with leftover dining chairs. Her son Tom runs the floor. Sell pieces that can be repaired. Most timber is British oak and walnut. Upholstery in West Yorkshire.

Before you drive over

Questions

Can I try the sofa if I have a dog?

Yes. Ours is usually under the desk. Bring a towel if yours sheds like ours.

Do you finance?

No. We will hold a piece for 48 hours on a card. That is as far as we go.

What if it does not fit up the stairs?

We measure on the phone. If we get it wrong, we take it back. Has happened twice in twenty years.
